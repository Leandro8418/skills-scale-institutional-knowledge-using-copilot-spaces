# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository brings together the core project management guidance used by OctoAcme to plan, execute, and improve cross-functional work. The goal is to provide a clear, central source of truth for teams so they can move from idea to delivery with shared expectations, defined ownership, and repeatable processes.

OctoAcme follows a structured lifecycle: initiation, planning, execution, release, and close/retrospective. In the initiation phase, teams validate the business need, identify stakeholders, define success metrics, and decide whether to proceed with planning. Planning translates the initiative into a backlog, milestone roadmap, dependencies, and a definition of done. Execution emphasizes day-to-day delivery through standups, demos, pull request practices, and active tracking of risks and blockers. When work is ready to ship, teams use a defined release and deployment process with smoke testing, rollback readiness, and stakeholder communication. After milestones or releases, retrospectives capture lessons learned and convert them into actionable improvements.

The process relies on clear cross-functional roles. Product managers define customer value and prioritize priorities, while project managers coordinate timelines, dependencies, communications, and risks. Developers build and validate the work, QA validates acceptance criteria and release readiness, and stakeholders provide input, trade-off decisions, and approvals. This combination of strong ownership and consistent communication helps teams align around outcomes while maintaining accountability.

Communication is a core practice across the lifecycle. OctoAcme uses daily standups, weekly delivery and PM syncs, milestone reviews, and periodic stakeholder updates to keep work visible and reduce surprises. Risk and dependency management happens through a simple risk register and escalation paths for blockers or incidents. Quality is reinforced through CI checks, testing, security scanning, pull request review standards, and release readiness gates. Together, these practices support faster delivery without compromising quality, transparency, or continuous improvement.

OctoAcme’s project management approach combines clear roles, lightweight documentation, regular communication, quality gates, and continuous improvement. The result is a practical operating model that helps teams move from idea to execution in a structured, transparent, and adaptable way.
